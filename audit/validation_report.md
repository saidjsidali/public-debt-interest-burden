## `large_annual_swings`

**Assessment.** Checked against the components. Falls after default or restructuring, where interest paid dropped (Lebanon 2020, Ghana 2023: from 7.4% to 3.1% of GDP; Sri Lanka 2024); a fall in revenue (Sri Lanka 2020); higher interest (Pakistan 2023: from 4.8% to 6.8% of GDP; Egypt 2024: from 6.8% to 8.8%); higher revenue (Nigeria 2024: from 7.3% to 10.8% of GDP; The Gambia 2017). Lao PDR's 2024 jump (interest from 0.8% to 3.7% of GDP) is not explained by the source and is treated as uncertain. A fall after a default means interest went unpaid, not that the debt became more sustainable.
